  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so tenants that write the day first get day and month flipped.
  • hint: tenants on booking-v3 write the day first, but their form prints MM/DD/YYYY. Their people type what the hint says. Fixing the parser does not fix this.
  • one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not by a month. The desk already marked these reports in desk-notes.json. The cause rule files them under parser by tenant, and they look like a timezone problem.

Why the merge hid it

  • Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser. The cause depends on the tenant, so the tag and the cause disagree on many reports. A tag is a guess.
  • The dedupe rule (decision 0001) merges on the subject line. These reports share subjects across different bugs.

Lessons

  • Merge on cause, not on subject. Count each cause once.
  • A footnote is a ticket nobody opened. Open it.
